芝加哥，1890 年代。一個女孩俱樂部在 Hull House 成立了。跟著蘿莎，從第一次聚會走到最後一次道別。

Chicago, the 1890s. Follow Rosa through one girls' club at Hull House, from its first meeting to its goodbye.
