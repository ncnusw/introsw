STORM OVER CLEAR CREEK

青溪颱風夜

一堂聽不懂的英文課、一場颱風、一次停電……醒來時，你站在一個山村的公車站。只有新來的社工小安看得到你。

A hard lecture in English, a typhoon, a blackout... and you wake up at a bus stop in a mountain village. Only Xiao An, the new social worker, can see you.

一個人玩：手機或電腦。兩人投影：一人用 8BitDo 或鍵盤操作，一人唸出對話、負責答題。兩人一支手機：一人操作、一人唸對話，每一段交換。
Solo on a phone or computer · Duo on a projector: one drives (8BitDo or keyboard), one reads aloud and answers · Duo on one phone: one taps, one reads; swap often.

換了手機？討論之後，輸入老師給的密碼，直接從「第二條路」開始。
New device? After the discussion, enter the password from your teacher to start Path 2.
