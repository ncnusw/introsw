See the English term. Say the Chinese and explain it. Then flip to check.

HOW TO PLAY 玩法

  1. Look at the English term. 看英文名詞。
  2. Out loud (or to a partner): say the Chinese, then explain it in one sentence or give an example. 說出中文，再用一句話解釋，或舉一個例子。
  3. Flip the card. Were you right? Got it or Again. "Again" cards come back later. 翻卡檢查。答對按「會了」，不會按「再一次」，稍後會再出現。
📱 PHONETap the card to flip. Swipe right = got it, left = again.
