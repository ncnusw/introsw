THE FAMILY NEXT DOOR

隔壁那一家

高中畢業的暑假，你想幫隔壁辛苦的陳家。你很熱心，辦法也很多……可是每一個都是用猜的。

The summer after high school, you want to help the struggling family next door. You have lots of ideas, but every one of them is a guess.

走進圖書館，讀懂研究，考過社工學士和碩士的畢業考（每一題都要答對），再回到和平巷。

Visit the library, learn research, pass your BSW and MSW exams (every question must be right), then come home to Peace Lane.

兩人一支手機：一人操作、一人唸對話，每一段交換。考試時兩個人一起討論再作答。
Pairs on one phone: one taps, one reads aloud; swap often. Decide exam answers together.

換了手機？輸入老師給的代碼，從中途開始。
New device? Enter the code from your teacher to start part-way.

和平巷、山城大學和所有角色都是虛構的。
Peace Lane, Hillside University and all their people are fictional.
